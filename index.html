Pose tracking / Motion art
03 / Generative pipeline

MotionCanvas

Transforms human movement from video into long exposure style digital artwork. The pipeline segments a subject, estimates body joints, tracks trajectories, and renders them as abstract motion trails.

Stack — Python, OpenCV, NumPy, MediaPipe
Output — High-resolution motion artwork and export-ready trails
Working MVP pipeline

About

Makingtheabstracttangible.

With an interest in artificial intelligence, computer vision, and spatial computing, I try to explore the intersection of emerging technologies and real-world applications. My work spans 3D reconstruction, Gaussian Splatting, 4D scene understanding, and interactive geospatial visualization.

Through projects like Lunar Ice Explorer, MotionCanvas, and experiments with the π⁴ foundation model, I aim at investigating how machines can perceive, reconstruct, and interpret the visual and physical world.

Working with technologies such as Python, JavaScript, CesiumJS, OpenCV, and machine learning frameworks, I try to explore new approaches to visual intelligence, motion understanding, and immersive 3D environments.

Alongside my technical projects, I am particularly interested in the application of AI in scientific research.

Driven by curiosity and experimentation, my approach revolves around learning through building, exploring emerging technologies, and turning ideas into working systems.
